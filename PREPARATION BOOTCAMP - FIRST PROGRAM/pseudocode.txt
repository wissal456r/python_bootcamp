Hangman game

1. Choose a random word.
2. Show one blank for each letter in the word.
3. Ask the player to guess a letter or the whole word.
4. If the letter is in the word, show the correct letters.
5. If the letter is wrong, add 1 penalty.
6. If the whole word is wrong, add 5 penalties.
7. If the penalties go over 12, the player loses.
8. Continue until the player guesses the word or loses.
